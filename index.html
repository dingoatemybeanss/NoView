About Surveillance Census

This is not just a map. It's a research instrument to prove a thesis: anonymity in modern cities is a myth.

Every pin represents a real surveillance camera I photographed, cropped, and geolocated while biking through Santo Domingo.

Why it matters: If Spider-Man can't stay anonymous in a city like this, neither can you.

Data governance: Raw pins are private. Only aggregated density grids are shown to the public. This tool is designed to critique surveillance, not enable avoidance of it.

Ethics & Limitations: This dataset is biased by my biking routes. It is not a complete census, but a controlled sample that demonstrates the density of surveillance infrastructure. I've gated raw coordinates to prevent dual‑use.

— Built for my college application, connecting technical fieldwork to my essay on mass surveillance.
